Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026. Last updated: 30 September 2026.

1.1 Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Biz Matrix (“BizMatrix”, “we”, “us”, “our”), operating the web application Biz Matrix – Business Structure Intelligence at www.bizmatrix.in (the “Platform”), collects, uses, stores, shares and protects your personal data.

This Policy is published in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it (the “DPDP Act”); the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (the “SPDI Rules”); and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

By using the Platform and giving consent where requested, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

1.2 Definitions

  • Personal Data: any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • Data Principal: you, the individual to whom the Personal Data relates.
  • Data Fiduciary: Biz Matrix, which determines the purpose and means of processing your Personal Data.
  • Assessment Data: the answers you enter in the 12-step assessment.
  • Report: the unlocked analysis, checklists, compliance calendar and downloadable PDF generated for you.

1.4 Payment data

Payments are processed by PCI-DSS certified third-party payment gateways, currently Cashfree Payments. Card, UPI, net-banking and wallet details are entered on the gateway’s secure page and handled directly by the gateway. Biz Matrix does not receive, see or store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVVs or bank credentials. We receive only a transaction reference, status and amount. The gateway’s own privacy policy governs its processing.

1.5 Lawful basis and consent

We process Personal Data on the basis of:

  1. Your consent, given by a clear affirmative action (for example, ticking a consent box or submitting the unlock form). Consent is specific to the purposes listed in this Policy.
  2. Legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act, such as fulfilling a service you voluntarily requested, complying with law, and preventing fraud.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see 1.9). Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and may prevent us from delivering or supporting the Report.

1.6 How we use your data

  • To generate and display your entity recommendation and Report;
  • To process payment, unlock access and email or WhatsApp your Report and confirmations;
  • To provide support and resolve grievances;
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, scraping and security incidents;
  • To analyse aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns to improve the Platform;
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your Personal Data. We do not use it for targeted third-party advertising. We will not send marketing messages unless you separately opt in.

1.7 Sharing and disclosure

We share Personal Data only as follows:

  • Payment gateway partners (Cashfree Payments) to process your payment;
  • Service providers acting on our behalf, such as email/WhatsApp delivery, hosting, analytics and error-monitoring providers, bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
  • Authorities, where required by law, court order or lawful government request;
  • Successors, in a merger, acquisition or restructuring, with notice to you where required.

Assessment Data used for analytics is aggregated and not linked to your identity.

1.8 Storage, location and cross-border transfer

We intend to store and process Personal Data on servers located within India. Where a service provider processes data outside India, this will be done only to the extent permitted by the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Central Government, with appropriate contractual safeguards.

1.9 Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the Personal Data we process and the identities of those with whom it is shared;
  • Correction and updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure of data that is no longer necessary for the purpose or where you withdraw consent, unless retention is required by law;
  • Grievance redressal (see the Grievance Redressal page);
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, email nexifydigital03@gmail.com from the address you used on the Platform. We may verify your identity before acting. We aim to respond within 30 days.

1.11 Security safeguards

We implement reasonable security practices consistent with the SPDI Rules and DPDP Act, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, access controls on a need-to-know basis, secure handling of gateway webhook signatures, logging and monitoring. No system is completely secure. In the event of a personal data breach we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by law.

1.12 Children

The Platform is intended for persons who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly process the Personal Data of children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

The Platform may link to government portals (for example MCA, GST, Udyam) and other sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be notified on the Platform with a revised “Last Updated” date. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
