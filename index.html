Questions

What is Biz Matrix?

Biz Matrix builds plain-language decision tools for Indian businesses. Today there are three: the Business Structure Matrix, which recommends a structure such as Proprietorship, LLP or Private Limited; the GST Registration Checker, which tells you whether GST registration is required; and the Income Tax Calculator, which works out income tax for Tax Year 2026-27.

Which tool should I use first?

If you have not yet set up your business, start with the Business Structure Matrix. If your business is already running or about to start selling, use the GST Registration Checker. To estimate the tax on your income or your business's profit, use the Income Tax Calculator.

Are the tools free?

The GST Registration Checker, its PDF report and the Income Tax Calculator are free. The Business Structure assessment and a preview of the result are free; the full structure report and PDF cost ₹49, one-time, inclusive of taxes.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Biz Matrix tools give structured, general guidance. Confirm current law and your specific facts with a qualified professional before you file or act.
