Questions

How long does the assessment take?

Most businesses answer 18 to 23 plain questions, which takes about 3 to 5 minutes. Questions that do not apply to you are skipped automatically, and your answers are saved on your device if you need to come back.

Which business structures does Biz Matrix compare?

Sole Proprietorship, Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, One Person Company, Private Limited Company, Public Limited Company (unlisted) and Section 8 Company.

How is the recommendation decided?

Legal knock-out rules run first and remove any structure the law does not allow for your answers. The structures that remain are scored on nine weighted factors such as liability risk, funding route and tax on profit. Every rule and weight is published on the How we decide page.

Does it list registrations, documents and compliances?

Yes. Your result lists the registrations and licences that apply to your entity, state, sector, staff and sales channels, the documents to collect for incorporation, and a compliance calendar from formation to recurring filings.

What is free and what is in the ₹49 report?

The assessment and a 35% preview of your result are free. The ₹49 one-time payment unlocks the full result on screen and the complete PDF report with every registration, document, compliance and action step.

Is this legal, tax or accounting advice?

No. Biz Matrix is structured decision support based on the law as verified on 6 November 2026. Confirm current requirements with a Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary or advocate before you file.
