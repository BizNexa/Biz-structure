Cookie & Tracking Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026.

4.1 What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, session storage, pixels) are small data items stored on your device to make websites work, remember choices and understand usage.

4.2 Categories we use

CategoryPurposeExamplesConsent
Strictly necessaryKeep the Platform functional, preserve assessment progress across the 12 questions, maintain session and payment-flow state, security and load balancingSession storage keys for answers and step; anti-fraud tokensNot optional; no consent required
PreferenceRemember settings such as display choicesLocal storage flagsOptional
AnalyticsMeasure traffic, device types, drop-off points and errors in aggregateAggregate analytics toolsOptional; only with your consent

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

4.3 Managing cookies

Use the cookie banner or preferences on the Platform to accept or decline optional cookies.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari: Settings → Privacy/Security → Cookies and site data).

Blocking strictly necessary cookies or storage may prevent the assessment from saving progress or the payment flow from working. Clearing site data will erase saved assessment progress.

4.4 Do Not Track

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” signals. Where technically feasible we will respect analytics opt-outs made through our preference controls.

4.5 Updates

We may update this Policy when our use of cookies changes. See also the Privacy Policy.
