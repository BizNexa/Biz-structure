Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Biz Matrix sells one digital product and does not ship physical goods. This page is the shipping policy for that product.

ItemDetails
ProductBiz Matrix full report for FY 2026–27: structure recommendation, document checklist, registration planner, compliance calendar, action plan and PDF
Price₹49, inclusive of all applicable taxes, charged in INR
Shipping charge₹0. There is no courier, postage or customs charge.
Delivery areaOnline, to the browser in which the assessment was completed. No street delivery.
Delivery timeUsually within a few minutes after PhonePe confirms the payment. If confirmation is delayed, use Check Payment Status on the assessment page.

How delivery works

  1. You answer the assessment and choose to unlock the full report.
  2. You pay ₹49 on PhonePe’s secure page.
  3. Biz Matrix checks the payment status with PhonePe. A successful check unlocks the full report on screen.
  4. A receipt with the order reference and a private access code is shown. Keep the code. It restores the purchase on another browser or device.
  5. You download the PDF from the report page. The report is not sent as an automatic email.

If delivery does not happen

  • A pending or failed payment does not unlock the report.
  • If the amount was debited and the report is still locked after 1 hour, email nexifydigital03@gmail.com with the order or transaction ID. We investigate within 24 hours on working days.
  • If we cannot deliver the paid report within 7 business days of a successful payment, you can claim a full refund under the Refund and Cancellation Policy. The refund goes to the original payment method.

What you need

A current browser, an internet connection, JavaScript, browser storage, and a PDF reader. Blocking storage or the payment redirect can stop delivery. Clearing site data removes the remembered purchase; use the access code or contact customer care with the order reference.

Customer care: nexifydigital03@gmail.com, Monday–Saturday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM IST.
