Shipping & Digital Delivery Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026.

6.1 No physical shipping

Biz Matrix offers only digital products. No physical goods are shipped, and no shipping charges or customs duties apply.

6.2 Delivery method and timeline

  • Instant on-screen unlock. After a successful payment, the gateway sends a server-to-server confirmation to Biz Matrix. On verification, the full action plan is unlocked on your screen, typically within seconds and in any event within 15 minutes.
  • Automated email delivery. A copy of the Report or PDF link and payment confirmation is emailed to the address you provided, usually within 10–30 minutes.
  • Downloadable PDF. Available from the on-screen dashboard after unlock.

6.3 If delivery fails

  • Check your spam or junk folder and confirm the email address you entered.
  • Do not close or refresh the payment page for a few minutes after payment, as the callback may still be processing.
  • If access is not unlocked or the email is not received within 1 hour, write to nexifydigital03@gmail.com with your order or transaction ID. We will re-deliver or manually unlock within 24 hours of verification on working days.
  • If we cannot deliver within the verification window of 5–7 business days, you are eligible for a refund under the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6.4 Technical requirements

A modern browser with JavaScript and session storage enabled, an internet connection and a PDF reader. Ad blockers or restrictive settings may interfere with payment redirection.

6.5 Ownership of delivery risk

Delivery to an incorrect email or phone entered by you is your responsibility, but we will make reasonable efforts to re-send to a corrected address after verification.
