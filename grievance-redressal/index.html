How to lodge a grievance

Email the Grievance Officer with:

  • your name and the email used at checkout;
  • the order or transaction ID, if there is one;
  • whether the complaint is about payment, refund, delivery, privacy or something else;
  • what you want us to do;
  • any screenshot or bank SMS. Do not send card numbers or UPI PINs.

What happens next

We acknowledge the email within 48 hours. Payment, refund and delivery complaints are resolved within 15 days. Every complaint is resolved within 30 days. We keep a record of the complaint and the outcome.

Refunds that are approved are returned in INR to the original PhonePe payment method, as described in the Refund and Cancellation Policy. If a complaint is still open after the resolution period, you may approach the consumer commission that has jurisdiction.
