Who must register

When GST registration becomes compulsory

Once your yearly turnover crosses the limit: ₹40 lakh if you sell only goods in most States, ₹20 lakh for services, ₹10 lakh in a few North-Eastern States. The checker applies the exact limit for your State.

Crossed the limit? Apply within 30 days. Your registration then counts from the day you became liable.

Registration is needed whatever your turnover if:

  • You sell to customers in another State or export, which requires registration whatever your turnover.
  • You sell through an online marketplace that collects TCS.
  • You will supply occasionally in a State where you have no fixed place of business (for example an event or exhibition).
  • You must pay GST yourself on some purchases (reverse charge), which requires registration.
  • You sell goods for someone else and bill the customer in your own name.
  • Your head office receives common service bills to pass the credit on to branches, which needs a separate ISD registration.
  • Your marketplace collects payments for sellers and must collect TCS, which needs a TCS registration.
  • Your platform pays GST on behalf of service providers (rides, stays, housekeeping, food delivery).
  • As a government body or PSU you must deduct GST TDS from supplier payments, which needs a TDS registration.
  • You have no fixed place of business or residence in India but will supply here occasionally.
  • You sell digital services from outside India to consumers in India.
  • You are taking over a GST-registered business as a going concern.

Documents required

What you will need to apply

PAN

PAN of the business, or of the proprietor. The legal name must match the PAN exactly.

Proof of business

Partnership deed, registration certificate or incorporation details, depending on the type of business.

Authorised signatory

Photograph, and a letter of authorisation or board resolution for anyone other than a proprietor.

Place of business

Ownership proof, rent agreement, consent letter or affidavit, based on how you occupy the premises.

Bank account

Passbook first page, statement or cancelled cheque, within 30 days of getting your GSTIN.

Aadhaar authentication

For the signatory and a promoter. It gets you the fast-track approval instead of a site visit.

FAQ

GST registration: common questions

What is the turnover limit for GST registration in India?

You must register once your aggregate turnover in a financial year crosses ₹40 lakh if you sell only goods (in most States), or ₹20 lakh if you supply services or both. The limit is ₹10 lakh in Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura. The ₹40 lakh goods limit does not apply in Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Puducherry, Sikkim, Telangana and Uttarakhand, where it is ₹20 lakh.

Who must register for GST even below the limit?

Anyone making inter-State taxable sales or exports, sellers on e-commerce marketplaces that collect TCS (with small-seller exemptions), casual traders at events in other States, persons paying GST under reverse charge, agents selling for others in their own name, input service distributors, TDS deductors and TCS-collecting platforms, non-resident suppliers and foreign suppliers of digital services to Indian consumers.

Do I need GST registration to sell on Amazon or Flipkart?

Generally yes. But small sellers of goods who sell only within one State, stay within the turnover limit this year and last year, and obtain a GST enrolment number are exempt; small service providers within ₹20 lakh are also exempt.

Is GST registration required for exporters?

Exports are inter-State supplies, so exporters of goods must register regardless of turnover. Small exporters of services within ₹20 lakh should confirm their position with a professional.

What counts in aggregate turnover?

All taxable, exempt and nil-rated sales, exports and inter-State supplies to your own branches, across India under one PAN. It excludes the GST itself and purchases on which you pay reverse charge. Sale of shares and other non-supplies is not counted.

How long does GST registration take?

Low-risk applications are approved electronically within 3 working days. Other complete applications are processed within 7 working days. If Aadhaar authentication is not done or the application is flagged, the premises are physically verified and approval can take up to 30 days.

What is the deadline to apply after crossing the limit?

Apply within 30 days of becoming liable. If you do, registration counts from the date you became liable. Casual and non-resident taxable persons must apply at least 5 days before they start.

Which documents are needed for GST registration?

PAN, proof of constitution (partnership deed, registration certificate, incorporation details), photographs and appointment proof of the authorised signatory, proof of the place of business based on ownership (owned, rented, consent or shared), and bank account proof. Aadhaar authentication of the signatory and a promoter speeds up approval.

What is the simplified GST registration under Rule 14A?

An optional route from 1 November 2025 for applicants whose monthly GST on sales to registered businesses stays within ₹2.5 lakh. With Aadhaar authentication, low-risk applications are approved within 3 working days. Only one such registration is allowed per PAN in a State.

Can I register for GST voluntarily?

Yes. Any business can register voluntarily even below the limit, for example to claim input tax credit or to sell to GST-registered buyers. You then have to file returns and charge GST like any registered person.
