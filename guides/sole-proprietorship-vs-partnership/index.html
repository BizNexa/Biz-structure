Sole proprietorship vs partnership firm: which suits your business?

The short answer

If you are starting alone, a sole proprietorship is the simplest and cheapest way to begin: there is no separate registration law, and the profit is taxed as your own income. If two or more people are starting together and sharing profit, you are a partnership: write a partnership deed and preferably register the firm. In both, the owners' personal assets can be used to pay business debts. If that worries you, compare an LLP.

When a sole proprietorship makes sense

  • You are the only owner and want to start quickly, with almost no setup cost.
  • The business is small and low-risk: freelancing, tuition, a small shop or consulting.
  • Your income is modest, so taxing it at your own slab rates costs less than a firm's flat 30%.
  • You are comfortable that your personal assets stand behind business debts.

When a partnership firm makes sense

  • Two or more people are investing and sharing the profit.
  • You want a simple structure with no MCA filings and low running costs.
  • The business carries modest risk, and every partner trusts the others, because each partner can bind the firm and all are liable for its debts.

What your partnership deed should cover

  • Name and address of the firm, and its business
  • Capital each partner brings in, and the profit and loss sharing ratio
  • Salary and interest on capital for working partners, within the limits allowed for tax
  • Roles, powers to sign and bank, and how decisions are made
  • Admission, retirement and death of a partner, and how the firm is dissolved
  • How disputes are resolved

An unregistered firm cannot file a court case against outsiders to enforce its contracts, so registering the firm with your State's Registrar of Firms is worth the small effort.

When to consider an LLP instead

Both a proprietorship and a partnership leave the owners personally liable. If your business takes on debt, large contracts, employees or premises, or works in a risky sector such as food, construction or healthcare, an LLP gives you limited liability with tax treatment similar to a firm. It does add yearly MCA returns. See LLP vs Private Limited for the next step up.

Questions

Is partnership registration compulsory?

In most States it is optional, but an unregistered firm cannot sue outsiders to enforce its contracts. Registering is usually worth it.

Does a sole proprietorship need GST registration?

Only if your turnover crosses the GST threshold or another rule applies, such as inter-State supply of goods or selling through an e-commerce operator. Check with our free GST Registration Checker.

Can I convert my proprietorship into a partnership later?

Yes. You bring in a partner, sign a partnership deed and transfer the business to the firm. The firm then gets its own PAN and registrations.

Which pays less tax?

For small profits, a proprietorship usually does, because your income is taxed at slab rates with the basic exemption and rebate. For larger profits, a firm's flat rate, plus deductible salary and interest to partners, can work out lower. Try our Income Tax Calculator.

How many partners can a firm have?

Between 2 and 50.
