Registration guide

How to register an LLP in India: step by step

The short answer

You register an LLP online on the MCA portal. Get Class 3 digital signatures for the designated partners, reserve a name, file the FiLLiP incorporation form with partner and office details, receive the Certificate of Incorporation with your LLPIN, PAN and TAN, then file the signed LLP agreement in Form 3 within 30 days.

Who can form an LLP

  • At least two partners. Partners can be individuals or companies; there is no maximum.
  • At least two designated partners, who must be individuals. At least one of them must be resident in India.
  • No minimum capital. Partners agree their contribution in the LLP agreement.

The steps

  1. Get digital signatures (DSC)

    Each designated partner needs a Class 3 digital signature certificate from a licensed certifying authority. It is used to sign the MCA forms.

  2. Choose and reserve a name

    The name must end with "LLP" or "Limited Liability Partnership" and must not match an existing company, LLP or trademark. Check the MCA name index and the trademark register, then reserve it through RUN-LLP, or propose it inside FiLLiP.

  3. Collect the documents

    Identity, address and PAN of every partner, and proof of the registered office. The full list is below.

  4. File FiLLiP

    FiLLiP is the incorporation form. It carries the partners' details, contributions, the registered office and their consents, and allots designated partner numbers (DPIN) to up to two designated partners who do not already have one. It is certified by a practising professional and filed with the fee based on contribution.

  5. Receive the Certificate of Incorporation

    Once approved, the Registrar issues the certificate with your LLPIN. The LLP's PAN and TAN are allotted along with it.

  6. Sign and file the LLP agreement

    Draft the agreement (capital, profit sharing, duties, admission and exit of partners), pay stamp duty as per your State, and file it in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. Late filing attracts an additional fee for every day of delay.

  7. Start operating

    Open a current account, bring in the agreed contributions, and take the registrations your business needs, such as GST, Shops & Establishments and Professional Tax.

Documents you need

For each partner

  • PAN card
  • Identity proof: Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or driving licence
  • Address proof: a bank statement, or an electricity, telephone or mobile bill, not older than two months
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email address and mobile number
  • Foreign nationals and NRIs: passport and overseas address proof, notarised or apostilled as required

For the registered office

  • A utility bill (electricity, water or gas) not older than two months
  • If rented: the rent agreement and a no-objection letter from the owner
  • If owned: proof of ownership

Prepared during filing

  • Consent of each partner to act
  • Subscription sheet with each partner's contribution
  • The LLP agreement, after incorporation

After registration: your yearly compliance

  • Form 11, the annual return, by 30 May each year
  • Form 8, the statement of accounts and solvency, by 30 October each year
  • Income tax return by the due date
  • Statutory audit only if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh
  • Form 4 whenever a partner joins or leaves, and Form 3 when the agreement changes

LLP filings carry a late fee for every day of delay, with no upper limit, so put these dates in a shared calendar from day one.

Common reasons for delay

  • A name that is too close to an existing company, LLP or trademark
  • Address proof or a utility bill older than two months
  • Names or dates of birth that differ between PAN, Aadhaar and the form
  • Missing owner's no-objection letter for a rented office
  • Forgetting Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation

Questions

Is there a minimum capital for an LLP?

No. Partners can agree any contribution. The government fee for incorporation depends on the total contribution.

Can I register an LLP myself?

Yes, the filing is online. You need digital signatures, and the incorporation form must be certified by a practising Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary or Cost Accountant.

Can NRIs or foreign nationals be partners?

Yes. At least one designated partner must be resident in India. Foreign partners need notarised or apostilled documents, and foreign investment rules apply.

Can a company be a partner in an LLP?

Yes. A company can be a partner and nominates an individual to act for it.

How long does registration take?

It depends on how quickly the MCA processes your forms and whether any resubmission is needed. Having the documents right first time is the biggest factor.
