Registration guide

Documents required for Private Limited Company registration

The short answer

For every director and shareholder you need a PAN, an identity proof, an address proof not older than two months and a photograph. For the registered office you need a utility bill not older than two months and, if rented, the rent agreement and the owner's no-objection letter. The company's MoA, AoA, director consents and declarations are prepared and filed online in the SPICe+ form.

Before you start

  • At least two directors, at least one of them resident in India, and at least two shareholders. The same people can be both.
  • At most 200 members. No minimum share capital.
  • Class 3 digital signatures for the directors and subscribers who sign the forms.
  • Director identification numbers (DIN) are allotted inside SPICe+ for up to three new directors.

Document checklist

Each director and shareholder (Indian)

  • PAN card
  • Identity proof: Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or driving licence
  • Address proof: a bank statement, or an electricity, telephone or mobile bill, not older than two months
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email address and mobile number

Foreign nationals and NRIs

  • Passport
  • Overseas address proof
  • Documents notarised, and apostilled or consularised as required

Registered office

  • A utility bill (electricity, water or gas) not older than two months
  • Rent agreement, if the premises are rented
  • No-objection letter from the owner
  • Proof of ownership, if owned

A company or LLP as shareholder

  • Its certificate of incorporation and PAN
  • Board resolution authorising the investment and a representative

Forms prepared during registration

  • SPICe+ Part A: name reservation (you can propose two names)
  • SPICe+ Part B: incorporation details, directors, shareholders, capital and registered office
  • e-MoA (INC-33) and e-AoA (INC-34): the company's objects and internal rules
  • INC-9: declarations by subscribers and first directors
  • DIR-2: each director's consent to act
  • AGILE-PRO-S: applications for GSTIN (if needed), EPFO, ESIC, a bank account and, in some States, Professional Tax

PAN and TAN for the company are applied for through SPICe+ and arrive with the Certificate of Incorporation.

After incorporation

  • Certificate of Incorporation with the CIN, PAN and TAN
  • Open the company's bank account and collect the share subscription money
  • Hold the first board meeting and appoint the first auditor within 30 days
  • Issue share certificates within two months of incorporation
  • File the commencement of business declaration (INC-20A) within 180 days
  • Set up statutory registers and a compliance calendar

Common reasons for rejection or delay

  • Proposed name too similar to an existing company, LLP or trademark
  • Utility bill or address proof older than two months
  • Mismatch between names on PAN, Aadhaar and the form
  • Unclear or unsigned owner's no-objection letter
  • Objects in the MoA that do not match the business activity

Questions

Is there a minimum capital for a Private Limited Company?

No. You can start with any share capital. Fees and stamp duty depend on the authorised capital and your State.

Can my home be the registered office?

Yes, with a recent utility bill and, if the home is not in your name, a no-objection letter from the owner.

Can the same person be a director and a shareholder?

Yes. Most small companies start with two people who are both directors and shareholders.

Do directors need a DIN before applying?

No. SPICe+ allots DINs to up to three new directors as part of incorporation.

Is a Private Limited Company the right choice for me?

It suits founders who plan to raise investment, issue ESOPs or keep profit in the business. If you will take most of the profit out, compare an LLP first.
