Partnership firm vs LLP: which should you choose?

The short answer

Both are taxed the same way: 30% on the profit, with partners' share not taxed again and partners' remuneration and interest deductible within limits. The big difference is liability: in a partnership firm partners are personally liable for the firm's debts; in an LLP liability is limited to the agreed contribution. A firm is cheaper and simpler to run and can use presumptive taxation; an LLP files two returns with the MCA each year but protects your personal assets.

When a partnership firm makes sense

  • A small, low-risk business among people who trust each other.
  • You want the lowest compliance and cost, with no MCA filings.
  • You want to use presumptive taxation instead of keeping full books.

When an LLP makes sense

  • The business takes on debt, large contracts, employees or premises, so limited liability matters.
  • You want a separate legal identity that continues when partners change.
  • You expect more than 50 partners in future.
  • You can manage two MCA returns each year and their due dates.

Converting a partnership firm into an LLP

An existing partnership firm can convert into an LLP under the LLP Act, carrying over its assets, liabilities and contracts. All partners must become partners of the LLP. The tax-neutral treatment of the transfer applies only if the conditions in the Income-tax Act are met, so check them before converting.

Questions

Which is better, a partnership firm or an LLP?

An LLP if you want limited liability and can manage two MCA returns a year; a partnership firm if the business is small, low-risk and you want the lowest cost and presumptive taxation.

Is the tax the same for a partnership firm and an LLP?

Yes. Both pay 30% plus surcharge and cess; partners' share of profit is not taxed again, and remuneration and interest to partners are deductible within the legal limits.

Can an LLP use presumptive taxation?

No. Presumptive taxation is available to individuals, HUFs and partnership firms, but not to LLPs.

Is registration compulsory for a partnership firm?

In most States it is optional, but an unregistered firm cannot sue outsiders to enforce its contracts, so registering is usually worth it.

Can a partnership firm be converted into an LLP?

Yes, under the LLP Act, with the consent of all partners. Check the Income-tax Act conditions for a tax-neutral conversion first.
